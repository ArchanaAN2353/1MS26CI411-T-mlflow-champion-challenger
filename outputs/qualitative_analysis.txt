QUALITATIVE COMPARISON OF MODEL A AND MODEL B

Dataset size: 20 examples
Model A: distilbert-base-uncased-finetuned-sst-2-english
Model B: textattack/bert-base-uncased-SST-2

Observation:
Both models produced identical predicted sentiment labels for all 20 evaluation examples.

Number of disagreements: 0

Analysis:
No disagreement examples were found in the evaluation dataset. Therefore, five disagreement cases cannot be provided without inventing results.

Although both models predicted the same labels, their confidence scores differed. This indicates that the models can have different levels of confidence even when their final predictions agree.

Conclusion:
Both models achieved 100% accuracy on this 20-example dataset. A larger and more diverse evaluation dataset is required to investigate whether their predictions differ on more challenging examples.
